## No Critical Failure Triggered

The run passes the central Physical Agent test: it refuses to substitute shipments or market share for productive robot hours, utilization, customer ROI or recurring service economics.

## What worked particularly well

1. **Physical Agent ledger behavior is explicit.** Deployed Fleet / Utilization / Productive Hours appear in the core mechanism rather than as an afterthought.
2. **Unknowns are preserved.** Customer-level industrial ROI, productive hours and software/model monetization are not fabricated.
3. **Scope discipline is strong.** Humanoid volume is separated from quadruped volume, and historical “global #1” is not used as an unconditional current fact.
4. **Financial-quality framing is balanced.** FY2025 profitability/cash are acknowledged, while 2026 Q1/H1 expense and OCF pressure are treated as a transition signal rather than proof of business failure.
5. **Policy scope is handled correctly.** Existing-model vs new-model FCC certification risk is separated.

## Main weaknesses

1. **Reverse Expectations remains underdeveloped.** Current market value is discussed, but not fully inverted into sustainable earnings / FCF / ROIC and operating requirements.
2. **Exact S1 unit-sales / ASP data should be exploited more fully.** The prospectus supports a more precise realized-ASP ledger than a rough >5,500 shipment denominator.
3. **Productive-hours commercialization bridge remains mostly a requested-data gap.** Future versions should explicitly track deployed fleet, uptime, productive hours, customer payback, repeat purchase, service/software revenue and maintenance burden.
4. **Falsification thresholds can be tied more tightly to productive deployment rather than generic shipment levels.
