## No Critical Failure Triggered

The full WorkBuddy report materially improves on the earlier summary excerpt. It explicitly separates shipment, sales, deployment and productive use, and refuses to fabricate robot-hours / industrial ROI.

## What worked particularly well

1. **Physical Agent ledger behavior appears.** The report does not stop at `units → share → revenue → PE`; it explicitly inserts Deployed Fleet, Utilization / Productive Hours and customer ROI, while marking them UNKNOWN where public evidence is absent.
2. **Scope discipline on humanoid volume.** It distinguishes 2025 humanoid shipments (>5,500 / company disclosure) from all-robot volume and does not merge them with quadruped shipments.
3. **Ranking discipline.** “Global #1” is not treated as an unconditional hard fact; it is identified as company / industry comparison subject to independent evidence.
4. **Financial-quality framing is balanced.** 2025 high gross margin, deduct-profit and OCF are acknowledged as real; 2026 Q1/H1 slowdown and OCF pressure are treated as an operating transition rather than automatic business failure.
5. **Software/model monetization is not invented.** Fundraising into embodied models is explicitly separated from proven ARR / software revenue.
6. **Policy scope is handled correctly.** Current models vs future/new-model FCC certification risk are separated.

## Main weaknesses

1. **Reverse Expectations remains incomplete.** The report says a RMB ~200bn market cap still requires high growth and model monetization, but does not back-solve sustainable future earnings / FCF / ROIC from current value.
2. **Exact S1 ASP data should be preferred over rough derived ASP.** The final prospectus discloses more precise unit-sales / realized-ASP data; the report instead derives an upper-bound ASP using >5,500 shipments. This is conservative but unnecessarily imprecise.
3. **Productive-hours ledger still lacks a commercialization bridge.** Because public data are missing, the report correctly outputs UNKNOWN, but future versions should explicitly request deployed fleet, uptime, productive robot hours, customer payback, repeat purchase and service/software revenue.
4. **Some current-period wording is stale inside the Ledger.** The report has already upgraded H1 2026 to FACT in the Reality table, but one Ledger row still says H1 “预计/报道”. This is minor freshness drift.
5. **Falsification thresholds can be tighter.** “千台级以上” is too low to test a company already shipping thousands of humanoids; thresholds should be tied to growth, realized ASP, productive deployment and cash conversion.

## v0.4 read-through

This case validates that v0.3.3 can transfer beyond optical/semiconductor-style company analysis into Physical AI without fabricating missing robot-economics data. The main cross-case gap remains valuation: current price is discussed, but not fully inverted into sustainable operating requirements. That strengthens the case for a future `Reverse Expectations` module if the same weakness appears across the remaining runtimes/cases.
